### Design Quality (weight: 0.35)
Does the interface look like a premium, award-winning application? Are the typography, spacing, and glassmorphism effects perfectly tuned?

### Originality (weight: 0.30)
Does it push the boundaries of standard dashboards? Are there creative leaps in how gradients, glows, and bento cards are applied?

### Craft (weight: 0.25)
Is the CSS clean, modular, and performant? Are animations smooth and sophisticated?

### Functionality (weight: 0.10)
Does it still display the necessary medical statistics, patient growth, and pending requests queue?
